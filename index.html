Our CPA Business Model

Cost Per Acquisition means commissions are linked to qualifying customer actions defined by the advertiser.

Advertiser Agreement

The advertiser agrees to a CPA rate and defines the qualifying acquisition and validation conditions.

Affiliate Commission

Corelink agrees an affiliate payout rate for each approved campaign and eligible affiliate.

Performance Validation

Conversions are recorded and checked against the advertiser's campaign rules before approval.

Transparent Reporting

Approved commissions, pending conversions and payment records are reported through the network.

CPA commissions are not guaranteed earnings. Only qualifying and approved acquisitions are eligible for payment.
